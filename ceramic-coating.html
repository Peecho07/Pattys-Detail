FAQ

Ceramic coating questions.

Why is paint correction included?

Anything on the paint when the coating goes on gets sealed in underneath it. We correct the paint first so you're protecting a clean, glossy finish rather than locking in swirls and contamination.

Will a ceramic coating stop scratches?

No. Coatings resist stains, oxidation and UV damage, but they can still be scratched by grit. For scratch and rock-chip resistance, paint protection film (PPF) is the better option.

Can you coat over paint protection film?

Yes. A ceramic coating can be applied over existing PPF.

How do I keep the coating looking its best?

Regular, proper washing. Every package includes a free maintenance wash after application, and our maintenance plans keep it in top shape.

Is the price fixed?

Prices shown are starting prices. Larger vehicles and paint that needs more correction cost more. Request a free estimate for an exact number.
