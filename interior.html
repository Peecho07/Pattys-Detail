FAQ

Interior questions.

How long does an interior detail take?

It depends on the vehicle's condition. Well-kept interiors take less time. We'll give you an estimate when you book.

Can you get every stain out?

Most, yes. Some deeply set stains, like long-dried grease or food, may be difficult or impossible to fully remove. We'll let you know before we start.

Do you charge extra for pet hair?

Heavy pet hair is priced by the amount present. Send a photo with your quote request for an accurate price.
